About Us

A builder since 19. Owner-led since 2003.

Matt Urbas discovered his passion for building at age 19, the first time he stepped onto a construction jobsite. After years of honing his skills, he opened First Class Construction in 2003 and has been dedicated to quality craftsmanship ever since.

He's known for building lasting relationships with his customers and takes pride in creating a positive experience on every project. Off the clock, you'll find him with his wife Natalie and their two daughters, or fishing and golfing.

Who are the contractors at First Class Construction, Inc.?

A skilled team of remodelers and contractors with over 20 years of experience, proudly serving families in Lake County, Ohio and nearby areas.

What does a luxury bathroom remodel include?

Premium finishes, fixtures and custom features such as double sinks, a custom shower, a tub and heated floors. Cost depends on size, materials and design; you'll receive an accurate quote after the in-home consultation.

What does a high-end home addition include?

Often structural changes, kitchen upgrades and bathroom remodels, and it may include expanding a basement or garage. Excavation may be required depending on scope.

What does it cost to build an ADU?

It depends on size, materials and finishes. FCC offers three ADU construction tiers. Schedule an in-home consultation for a precise quote.
